# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Demo viewers first: hackathon judges, recruiters, and stakeholders opening the public HF Space (prajval007/smartcropadvisory) on a laptop in a bright room, deciding within ten seconds whether this is a credible product. Always farmer-credible second: Indian farmers on budget Android phones, outdoors, on patchy networks, reading one of 10 languages (English + 9 Indic scripts). Design choices may optimize for the first audience but must never be indefensible for the second.

## Product Purpose

KisanOS is an agronomy decision instrument. Each tab answers one field question with one measured answer: which crop to sow (crop), what is wrong with a leaf (disease), when to sell (market), how much to water (irrigation), what insects are audible in the field (acoustic), and whether the field is safe today (field watch). Success: a first-time viewer says "a real product company built this" and a farmer gets a clear answer without reading a manual.

## Brand Personality

Engineered, precise, quietly confident. A precision hardware company that happens to make farm software. Lowercase voice, monospace-labeled data, restraint over decoration; the drama lives in the instruments, not the chrome.

## Anti-references

- Glassmorphism cards over stock photography (the previous KisanOS UI).
- Generic dark "AI dashboard" SaaS: gradient heroes, hero-metric stat cards, identical card grids, glowing purple.
- Cartoon agritech: leaf icons everywhere, green gradients, emoji-led UI.

## Design Principles

1. The data is the hero. One focal instrument per screen; everything else recedes.
2. Restraint reads premium. Monochrome paper and ink, one engineered green, accent coverage at or below 10% of any screen.
3. Motion explains, then delights. Choreography (reveals, draws, ticks) over decoration; every animation has a narrative job.
4. Engineered, not decorated. Hairline rules, numbered sections, mono microcaps, grid discipline. No glass, no photos, no gradients.
5. Every state designed. Loading, empty, and error are part of the show, never browser defaults.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for all text on both paper and carbon surfaces. Full prefers-reduced-motion support: all choreography collapses to instant states. Touch targets at least 44px. The 9 Indic scripts are first-class via per-language display font swaps; lowercase styling is applied via CSS (text-transform) so Indic scripts and screen readers are unaffected.
